1114 sequence: 12h00 to 12h45

1114 = 1 + 1 + 1 + 4. Three 1s are three confirmations, the 4 is the entry move. Do not trade the first 1.

12h00, 1: first sweep. Price taps S/R and the first candle forms. Wait, no trade.
12h15, 1: retest. Close + Retest, FVG + Order Block, BOS/CHoCH. Need Total 15+, Repo 5+, Order Flow 2+. Still no trade.
12h30, 1: all align. Imaginary Candles, MicroFish, Gen AI and Order Flow align. Predictor 70%? Armed.
12h45, 4: perfect entry. Trade only if every check passes. Otherwise skip and wait for New York 15h00-17h00.

Trading 12h00 alone is retail. Waiting for all four steps is quant style.
